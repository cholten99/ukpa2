What it's like

A monthly low pressure vanilla social and discussion group meeting in a private room in a local Ipswich pub. There are small group discussions, with 6-10 ice breaker poly based questions every month to help facilitate conversation. Open to experienced ENM people and people looking to understand more about ethical non-monogamy.

Plan for the evening

  • 19:30 – 20:30: Arrivals / getting settled in
  • 20:30 – 21:30: Small group discussions
  • 21:30 – 22:30: More chat and drinks

Brief history

Running since May 2022, this is a friendly local event to support all people wherever they are on their polyamory journey. The event attracts approximately 20-30 people every month.

How to join

Events are run via FetLife. The group meets every fourth Tuesday of the month.

Community rules

  • Be kind
  • Respect all
  • Don't attend if unwell

Where it's based

The Stables private room, The Halberd, 15 Northgate Street, Ipswich, IP1 3BY.
