London Poly Pub

The London Polyamory Meetup Group's monthly pub social: an informal evening for anyone interested in polyamory in its many forms. It has been running for more than 25 years and regularly draws 60 people or more.

Meetup group

Type: In person

Area covered: London (central London venue)

Meets: First Tuesday of every month, from 6pm

Cost: Free; buy your own drinks. A voluntary £1 towards the group's Meetup costs is welcome.

Accessibility: Wheelchair accessible, with a ramp and a buzzer for staff at the entrance steps

Last updated: November 2026

What it's like

A relaxed, sociable evening in an ordinary pub. People talk about anything from polyamorous life to the weather. It's a social group, not a sex or swingers group, and it isn't a place to look for partners.

The group usually gathers towards the back of the pub. It's a big, busy meet, so if you'd prefer somewhere quieter for your first visit, try Poly Coffee (below).

How to join

Events are listed on the London Polyamory Meetup Group. To join the group you answer two short questions, and the organisers may get in touch before approving you. If you're a couple or more, each person should join with their own account.

Guidelines

  • The meet isn't for cruising or picking up partners. Applications that look like sex-seeking or "unicorn hunting" are turned down.
  • Don't message other members unless you've met them at a meetup and had a proper conversation.
  • Report unwanted "let's meet" or predatory messages to the organisers.
  • Researchers and journalists are asked not to question people at meetups. Contact the organisers instead.

Accessibility

The pub is wheelchair accessible. There are a couple of steps at the entrance, with a buzzer to call staff and a ramp. If you'd like help on the night, let the organisers know in advance through the Meetup group.

Where it's based

The Sir John Oldcastle (Wetherspoon), 29–35 Farringdon Road, London EC1M 3JF, a short walk from Farringdon station. Check the Meetup group before you go in case the venue changes.
