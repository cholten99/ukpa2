Privacy Policy

Marketing

The UKPA will never send you marketing materials for anything other than matters directly related to the organisation itself.

What are your data protection rights?

The UKPA would like to make sure you are fully aware of all of your data protection rights. Every person is entitled to the following:

  • The right to access – request a copy of your personal data.
  • The right to rectification – request that we correct information you believe is inaccurate, or complete information you believe is incomplete.
  • The right to erasure – request that we erase your personal data.
  • The right to restrict processing – request that we restrict the processing of your personal data.
  • The right to object – object to our processing of your personal data.
  • The right to data portability – request that we transfer the data we have collected to another organisation, or directly to you.

If you make a request, we have one month to respond. To exercise any of these rights, please email trustees@ukpolyamory.org .

Cookies and advertising tracking

Cookies are small text files that a website places in your browser, for example to recognise you on a later visit.

Every page on our website includes the Google Ads tag, provided by Google. When you visit a page, it sets cookies in your browser and sends Google information about your visit, including your IP address, the page you looked at, the page you came from, and details of your browser and device. We use it to measure how well any adverts we run for the UKPA work, for example how many people who click an advert go on to visit particular pages. We are not running any adverts at the moment, but may do again in future.

Google handles this information under its own privacy policy. You can control how Google uses information for advertising in My Ad Center, and you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings or with a tracker blocker. Our website works without them.

Our map of UK groups shows map images from OpenStreetMap. When you view it, your browser fetches those images from the OpenStreetMap Foundation's servers, which receive your IP address. They don't set tracking cookies; see the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy. Most of our group pages show their area or venue on an OpenStreetMap map in the same way.

A few group pages show the venue on an embedded Google Map instead. Viewing one of those pages sends Google your IP address and details of the page, and Google may set cookies; this is also covered by Google's privacy policy.

Some third-party services we link to (for example, donation processors) may use their own cookies.
